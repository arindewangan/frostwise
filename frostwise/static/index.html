Frostwise

Hyperlocal frost-date predictions from TabPFN, the open-source tabular foundation model — trained on real ERA5 climate history — plus a planting calendar anchored to your first and last frost.

Try:

How it works

1. We pull 20 years of daily minimum temperatures for 80 global locations from the Open-Meteo archive (ERA5 reanalysis, free & open).
2. For each location we compute the median first/last frost day-of-year (2005–2024).
3. TabPFN (open-source, PriorLabs/TabPFN) learns the mapping from climate features → frost dates in-context — no gradient training, just real inference at request time.
4. Your location's climate fingerprint goes through the same pipeline; the predicted frost dates anchor a rule-based planting calendar for 12 common crops.

Predictions are statistical estimates from historical climate, not weather forecasts. Microclimates (urban heat, valleys, altitude pockets) can shift real frost by days.
